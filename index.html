US Apparel Import Duty by Country of Origin, October 2026

As of October 26, 2026, a typical cotton knit garment pays about 12.5% US duty from Colombia, about 29% from Vietnam, about 26.5% from India and Bangladesh, and about 36.5% from China (36 to 52% across HTS codes). Qualifying goods from Mexico, Canada and Central America pay 0%. No 2027 rates have been published.
